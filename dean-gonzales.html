Dean Gonzales · AMD Senior Fellow

Thursday, January 28Networking 6:45 PMSpeaker 7:00 PMECCR 200, CU Boulder

Topic preview

One high-speed channel, from silicon to system

Explore how engineers preserve signal integrity across the entire electrical path—from silicon and advanced chiplet packages through PCBs and system interconnects—as modern AI processors push the limits of bandwidth and complexity.

Think your 1 Gb/s home fiber connection is fast? A single 400 Gb/s data-center link can carry 400 times as much data per second. What takes your home connection nearly seven minutes to transfer could cross that link in just one second. Now imagine maintaining signal integrity across the silicon, packages, and PCBs that make those speeds possible!

This introductory preview frames the challenge of designing an electrical path as one complete system, rather than treating each part in isolation. Package routing, PCB interconnects, channel loss, reflections, crosstalk, and receiver design all shape whether a high-speed link will work.
