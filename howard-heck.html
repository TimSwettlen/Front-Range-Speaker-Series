Topic preview

Taking high-speed signals beyond the board

Explore the signal integrity challenges of moving data between boards and systems as electrical interfaces approach 400 Gb/s per lane and beyond.

At 400 billion bits per second, each bit represents just 2.5 picoseconds. In that time, even light traveling through a vacuum covers less than one millimeter—about the thickness of a credit card. Imagine designing connectors and cables to preserve signals at that speed!

The discussion is expected to introduce how connectors, cables, and complete electrical channels influence loss, reflections, crosstalk, and design tradeoffs as data rates increase.
